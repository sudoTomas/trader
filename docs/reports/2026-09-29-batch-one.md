### Lane B — #23, #11

- Unattended opens above `risk_approval_notional_usd` clamp to that cap and execute. Parking happens only when `TRADER_RISK_PARK_ABOVE_USD` sits strictly above the cap (default `0`, parking off). A clamp under the notional floor is a deny. Exits are not clamped.
